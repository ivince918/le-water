## Design Specificity Verdict
The WORDS are authored for this store ($0.375/gal member price, "balance follows your phone number," the JugMark logo, real neighborhood Yelp quotes). The PICTURES are authored for the category — generic full-bleed droplet hero + stock gallery (incl. a competitor-adjacent Flow bottle) the code itself flags as placeholder. Palette (ink/blue/cyan) is competent but is the default "clean water/clean SaaS" story — no warmth, no family, nothing ownable. Site is currently carried by copy + jug mark, dragged toward interchangeable by stock imagery.

Deterministic scan (DEGRADED): 1 finding — `overused-font` (Inter, index.html:22), warning/slop severity. Taste call, not a defect. Contrast rules did NOT run, so Sam's low-opacity-text concern is unverified by the detector.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Reviews section contradicts itself → reads as fabricated trust.** Cards cite "Verified Yelp review," the aggregate now says "188 Google reviews," and every card shows 5 solid stars against a 4.1 headline. (Note: the Google/188 half was just introduced this session — it now clashes with the Yelp-labeled cards.) On a family business, perceived honesty is the moat. Fix: one truth per element — relabel cards to "Verified customer review" or source them from Google; stop blanket-5-starring. Command: `clarify`.

**[P1] Mobile nav is effectively empty.** The 5 section links AND the "Find a store" chip are all `hidden md:*` — on phones the sticky nav shows only the logo. No hamburger, no way to jump to Balance/Stores. Repeat balance-checkers on mobile (majority traffic) must scroll the whole page. Fix: add a mobile menu (or at least a persistent "Balance" + "Stores" affordance). Command: `adapt`.

**[P1] Stock imagery undercuts every claim it sits beneath.** Hero droplet, review gallery (stock strangers + non-owned bottle), bottle cards. Differentiation is "local, family, same water" — stock says the opposite. Three real phone photos (storefront w/ sign, fill station mid-pour, jug on counter) beat every motion effect. Ship the hero swap first; also fixes JSON-LD `image` (currently favicon) + missing og:image. Command: `adapt`.

**[P2] Intro loader taxes returning balance-checkers.** 2.85s blocking splash every load (only bypassed by reduced-motion). The repeat action is a utility lookup. Fix: show once per session via sessionStorage; skip when URL has `#balance`. Command: `optimize`.

**[P2] Low-opacity ink likely fails WCAG AA.** Helper text and eyebrows at `text-[#0A1220]/40`, `/35`, footer `text-white/40`, `/35`. Detector couldn't verify (degraded), but several are plausibly under 4.5:1. Also: Stars component has no accessible label; decorative quote glyph not aria-hidden. Command: `audit`.

## Questions to Consider
