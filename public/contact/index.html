Have we changed names?

Yes. All three stores now trade as Le Water Store. They opened at different times under different names, and you may still find the old ones on directories, older receipts or your phone's saved contacts. Same owners, same stores, same water:

  • The Water Spot — now Le Water Store, North Fremont, 35762 Fremont Blvd. Our original store, open since 1998.
  • Le Pure Water — now Le Water Store, Central Fremont, 39409 Fremont Blvd.
  • Lion Pure Water — now Le Water Store, Newark, 39131 Cedar Blvd, in Lion Supermarket Plaza.

Nothing about the water or the prepaid plans changed with the name. Your gallon balance still follows your phone number to any of the three.

What we sell

We are a water store, not a delivery service — you come to us and we fill your container at the counter while you wait. We refill purified reverse-osmosis water and mineral-rich alkaline water by the gallon, and we sell empty bottles if you need one: 1 gallon, 3 gallon, and 5 gallon, including a 5 gallon with a built-in spigot. Every bottle is BPA-free and sized to fit a standard water dispenser or crock. Ask at the counter for current bottle pricing.

Looking for a water refill station near you in Fremont or Newark? All three stores also have a self-serve water vending machine outside, running 24 hours a day on cash, so you can fill up after we close.

Prepaid balances

Prepaid gallon plans are tracked by phone number and work at all three stores. You can look up your balance on our homepage, or just ask at the counter.

Questions about your account

Call the store you usually visit — they can see your balance and recent fill-ups. For anything to do with the information we hold about you, see our privacy policy.
